Time division multiplexing

Session 2, Part 3. Frames A, B and C step onto one link in turn, then step off to the right destination.

Back to the INT3303 simulation index

Each source hands its frames to the multiplexer. The multiplexer puts one frame at a time on the link, always in the same order, and the demultiplexer at the far end counts them off in the same order to decide who each one belongs to.

What to look for
  1. Only one frame is ever entering the link at a time, and the order never changes: A, B, C, A, B, C.
  2. Every frame uses the full width of the link. Nothing is divided in frequency here, only in time.
  3. The demultiplexer has no label to read. It knows the frame is B's purely because it is the second one in the cycle, which is why TDM needs synchronisation.
  4. Switch B off. Its turn still comes round and an empty frame goes out. The utilisation figure falls, and that gap cannot be given to A or C.
Built for INT3303. The frame and slot structure follows Beard & Stallings, section 2.5.
