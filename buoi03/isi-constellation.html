Inter-symbol interference on the constellation

A late copy of the previous symbol does not corrupt one bit. It pulls a whole constellation point out of place. This page runs that calculation for 16-QAM: move the sliders and watch the cloud of received points spread out.

Back to the INT3303 simulation index

The channel has two paths: the direct one, and a weaker echo that arrives later. The receiver integrates over each symbol slot, so once the echo spills into the following slot, the sample of the current symbol carries a piece of the previous one.

The I branch against time. Solid blocks are the direct signal, pale blocks are the echo. The darker overlap is where the echo of one symbol sits inside the next symbol's slot, and that overlap is exactly what is added to the received sample.

What this page computes

  • Two-path channel, rectangular pulses, a receiver that integrates over one whole symbol slot. Write the echo as h = a·e^{jφ} and the fraction of it that spills into the next slot as β = τ·Rs.
  • The sample for symbol k is y[k] = s[k]·(1 + h·(1 − β)) + s[k−1]·(h·β). The first term is the wanted symbol, distorted; the second is inter-symbol interference.
  • At β = 0 the echo stays inside its own slot: it changes amplitude and phase, and interferes with nothing. Undo the main tap, turn the noise floor off, and the EVM is exactly zero. That is why a strong echo is not necessarily a harmful one.
  • Hold the delay τ fixed and raise the symbol rate Rs: β rises with it and the cloud spreads. The environment did not change, only the rate did.
  • The noise floor is added Gaussian noise set by Es/N0, with the symbol power normalised to one. This is the noise margin from part 2.
  • EVM is the RMS error vector divided by the RMS signal. Symbol errors are counted by nearest-neighbour decision over 2000 symbols.

Four things worth trying

  • Set a strong echo of 0.70 with zero delay and the noise floor off. The constellation stays perfectly clean. Ask the class why.
  • Hold τ at 200 ns and drag the symbol rate from 0.25 to 4 Msym/s. This is the slide Short symbols suffer more, seen on the constellation.
  • With the noise floor off, raise β: the cloud spreads a long way and the symbol error count stays at zero, because the interference has not yet crossed half the decision distance. Turn the noise floor on at 22 dB and repeat: errors appear far sooner. Inter-symbol interference spends the noise margin before it causes errors on its own.
  • Clear the main-tap checkbox. The whole constellation rotates and shrinks. An equalizer fixes that part, and does nothing about the cloud.
