What the channel does, and what the noise does

Slide 22 lists everything that happens to a signal on its way across a room. Two of those things move a symbol on the constellation diagram, and they move it in two completely different ways. Switch them on one at a time and watch one symbol.

We follow one single symbol. The three faint rings are only the other values a QPSK symbol is allowed to take; exactly one value is being sent, over and over again. The left panel is what leaves the transmitter and the right panel is where that symbol lands at the receiving antenna.

Two effects, and the difference between them

What it does to the symbol The thing to notice
Channel C Turns it by a fixed angle and changes its length by a fixed factor. It does the same thing every time. Keep the noise off and send the symbol a thousand times: it lands in exactly the same wrong place a thousand times. The symbol has moved, but nothing about it has become uncertain.
Noise n Pushes it a little further, in a direction and by an amount that are drawn afresh on every single transmission. It does something different every time. That is why the trail of past receptions is a cloud rather than a point, and why the landing place can no longer be predicted even if you knew everything about the room.
The room changing C itself drifts, so the fixed turn and the fixed length stop being fixed. The symbol wanders even with the noise switched off. Whatever was true about the channel a moment ago is slightly out of date.
The distinction matters more than it looks. One of these two is a property of the room and is the same for every symbol that crosses it. The other is drawn fresh for every symbol. Session 5 turns on exactly this difference, so it is worth making students say which is which before moving on.
Two things deliberately left out. What the receiver then does about any of this is a separate question and is not shown here. And movement also shifts the frequency of the signal, which is the Doppler effect listed on slide 22; that is a different mechanism from the drift of C and it is not drawn on this page either.
